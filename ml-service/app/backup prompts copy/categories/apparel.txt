CATEGORY OVERLAY — APPAREL

When grading apparel, weight these category-specific factors:
- Fabric condition: pilling, fading, stretching, shrinkage, thinning.
- Stains and odors: any discoloration, sweat marks, or set-in stains are major signals.
- Seams and hardware: loose seams, missing buttons, broken zippers, damaged closures.
- Care tags and brand labels: presence and legibility (use OCR text); missing or
  altered labels are a counterfeit/authenticity signal.
- Wear at high-stress points: collar, cuffs, underarms, knees, elbows.

Required evidence fields for apparel: front view, back view, close-up of any defect,
close-up of brand/care label. If the care label or brand label is not verifiable,
add "brand_label" to missingEvidence.
