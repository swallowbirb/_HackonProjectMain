CATEGORY OVERLAY — ELECTRONICS

When grading electronics, weight these category-specific factors:
- Functional verification: powers on, screen/display intact, ports and buttons work.
  Functionality is the dominant grading factor; non-functional => D.
- Screen/body condition: cracks, dead pixels, deep scratches, dents.
- Serial number / model label: extract via OCR; verify it is present and consistent
  with the catalog product. Missing or mismatched serial is an authenticity signal.
- Accessories and power: presence of charger/cables/original adapter.
- Battery / wear indicators where visible.

Required evidence fields for electronics: powered-on screen photo, serial/model label
close-up, all ports/sides, included accessories. If the device cannot be confirmed to
power on, add "function_verified" to missingEvidence and cap confidence at medium.
