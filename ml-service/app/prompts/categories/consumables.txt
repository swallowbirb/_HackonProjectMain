CATEGORY OVERLAY — SEALED CONSUMABLES (skincare, cosmetics, pharmaceutical, supplements, grocery).

When grading sealed consumables, weight these category-specific factors:
- These are hygiene- and safety-sensitive. The factory seal is the decisive signal.
- If the seal / shrink-wrap is intact AND packaging is undamaged AND not expired: may resell.
- If the seal is broken, the product is opened/used, tampered, or past expiry: it is NOT
  resellable for safety/regulatory reasons -> Grade D, routingHint = liquidate, ALWAYS.
- Expiry / batch label: extract via OCR; verify it is present, legible, and not past date.

Required evidence fields for consumables: factory seal / shrink-wrap close-up, packaging
condition, expiry/batch label close-up. If the expiry label is missing or illegible,
add "expiry_label" to missingEvidence and liquidate to be safe.
