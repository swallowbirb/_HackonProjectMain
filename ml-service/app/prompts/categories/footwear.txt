CATEGORY OVERLAY — FOOTWEAR

When grading footwear, weight these category-specific factors:
- Sole wear: tread depth, heel wear, separation of sole from upper. Heavy sole wear
  is a strong downgrade signal.
- Upper condition: creasing, scuffs, stains, discoloration, material cracking.
- Interior: insole wear, odor, heel-cup breakdown.
- Brand markings and authenticity: logo placement, stitching quality, box/label OCR.
- Pairing: confirm both shoes present and matching.

Required evidence fields for footwear: top view of both shoes, sole view of both shoes,
close-up of heel/toe wear, brand marking close-up. If sole wear cannot be assessed,
add "sole_condition" to missingEvidence.
