STEP 1 — VERIFY THE SET FIRST (this gates everything else):
  (a) Do the photos show the SAME KIND of item as the catalog product below?
      If clearly unrelated to the product (a pet, a person, food, a random scene),
      the field FAILS.
  (b) Does the SET satisfy this field's expected subject and validity criteria?
      For multi-view fields ("front and back", "both sides"), all required views
      must be present across the set — but they can be in DIFFERENT photos, e.g.
      one photo shows the front and another shows the back is fine. List any
      missing views in `missing_views`.
  (c) Is the evidence original — not a screenshot, not a photo of a screen, not
      the product's own catalog/marketing image?
For baseline angle fields (front/rear/left/right) the goal is simply to confirm
the product is genuine and present from that angle.

If STEP 1 fails on any point: accepted = false, write a single, concise,
field-level reupload_reason that tells the user what to add or replace. Still
fill `per_photo` with one entry per provided photo so the UI can flag which
specific photos were the problem (e.g. usable=false on a screenshot).
